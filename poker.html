How a hand plays

  1. BlindsThe two players left of the dealer put in the small and big blind, so there's always something to win.
  2. The dealEveryone gets two private cards. First round of betting.
  3. The flopThree shared cards go face up in the middle. Betting round.
  4. The turnA fourth shared card. Betting round.
  5. The riverThe fifth and final shared card. Last betting round.
  6. ShowdownMake your best five-card hand from your two cards and the five shared ones. Best hand takes the pot.

Your moves

  • Check pass, if no one has bet
  • Bet put chips in
  • Call match the bet
  • Raise bet more
  • Fold give up the hand
  • All in everything on the line

House rules

  • €10 buy-in. Everyone starts with the same stack of chips.
  • The dealer button moves one seat left after every hand.
  • Blinds double every 30 minutes, so the game keeps moving.
  • Lose all your chips and you're out. Bring snacks to the survivors.
  • Last player with chips takes the whole pot.
  • Tied hands split the pot.
  • Nihar's rulings are final. Even when they're wrong.
